🌲 BLOCKCRAFT 3D 🐺

A first-person blocky wilderness. 4 children are trapped in cages guarded by wolves, red-eyed wolves, bears, and lions.

Chop wood, mine iron, smelt it at the Machine, and feed the campfire to 100. Open chests (yellow), red (rare) and blue (hammer!) to grow your bag.

By day hunt rabbits; by night deer attack — use the flashlight, or defeat & feed one to tame it.

Goal: survive to Day 200 to win — rescuing all 4 kids (press R near a cage) is a bonus, keep playing after! Your 🏠 safe base (blue ring by the campfire) keeps enemies out. Craft a 🗺️ Map to reveal cages; get a flashlight at the 🗼 light house.
